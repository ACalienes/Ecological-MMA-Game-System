The coaching gold worth keeping

Miss a combo, you lose, no exceptions

Alex had to correct this mid-class: if offense throws a full combination and lands nothing, offense loses the exchange outright, even if the defense wasn't very good. A discipline rule against flinging and hoping.

Hand control is always live unless stated otherwise

Said twice, in two different games. This is a standing default across nearly every striking drill and has never been written down anywhere in the system. Worth locking in as a rule, the same way the above-the-knee connection law is.

The wall is an ally, not just a boundary

In the turtle escape, Leo is coached to glue to the wall on hands and knees, then use it to climb upright while peeling off Alex's hooks, rather than fighting the escape flat on the floor.
